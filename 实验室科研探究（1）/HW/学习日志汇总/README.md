## 作业要求

格式请参考附件的《学习日志汇总模板》，包含以下内容：
 1. 本学期探究单元学习期间的所思所想所获，每个单元字数500以上（共8个单元）；

**Please refer to the attached "Learning Log Summary Template" for the format. It should include the following contents:
 1. Thoughts, reflections and gains during the inquiry unit learning period of this semester, with each unit having more than 350 words (a total of 8 units);
 2. A summary of the overall gains and experiences from the course, approximately 180 words.**
